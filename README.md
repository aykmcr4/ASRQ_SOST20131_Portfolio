## Why reproducibility matters
I believe reproducibility matters for 2 main reasons, first on a personal aspect and then a community aspect. I personally believe that if the work that I produce is reproducible then it can be verified by other users with a similar interest in the field and enhance my own work in turn, and by sharing interest it can help build trust and connections with people who want to work towards a similar cause. And most importantly reproducible research supports future studies by providing a foundation for others to build upon, ensuring that my research findings are actionable and applicable in other's context.  Reproducibility is not just a technical requirement; it is a commitment to scientific excellence and integrity, which is fundamental to the trustworthiness of research and the effective utilization of efforts.
